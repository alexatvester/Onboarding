# Follow-up Email Template

**Subject:** Re: [original subject line]

---

## Variant A — After no response (5-7 days)

Hi {investor_first_name},

Just wanted to bump this to the top of your inbox. I know things get busy — would love to find a time to chat about {company_name} if you're interested.

{Optional: 1 sentence of new traction/news since last email.}

Happy to work around your schedule.

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## Variant B — After initial interest but no next step

Hi {investor_first_name},

Great chatting the other day — wanted to follow up with {what you promised: deck / data room / metrics}.

{Link or attachment as discussed.}

Let me know if any questions come up as you review. Happy to schedule a deeper dive whenever works.

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## Variant C — After meeting, waiting on decision

Hi {investor_first_name},

Thanks again for the conversation on {date}. Really enjoyed discussing {specific topic from meeting}.

Wanted to check in on timing — we're {update on where the raise stands, e.g., "moving toward closing by end of month"}. Would love to have {firm} involved.

Happy to provide any additional info that would be helpful for your process.

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## Notes
- Follow up max 2x before moving on (3 total touches)
- Always add value or new context in follow-ups — don't just "bump"
- Reference specific prior conversation details to show this isn't mass email

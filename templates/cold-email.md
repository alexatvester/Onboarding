# Cold Email Template

**Subject:** [PERSONALIZED SUBJECT — reference their portfolio, sector focus, or recent activity]

---

Hi {investor_first_name},

{OPENING — 1 sentence connecting to something specific about them: a recent investment, a talk they gave, a portfolio company, or a shared interest.}

I'm building {company_name} — {one_liner}. {1-2 sentences on traction or why now.}

We're raising {raise_amount} to {primary use of funds}, and given your focus on {their_focus_area}, I thought this could be a strong fit.

Would love to share more — happy to send our deck or jump on a quick call. What works best?

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## Personalization Notes
- Always reference something specific about the investor (not generic flattery)
- Lead with traction if strong, lead with vision/market if early
- Keep under 150 words
- No attachments in first email — offer to send deck

# Thank You Email Template

**Subject:** Great connecting — {company_name}

---

Hi {investor_first_name},

Really enjoyed our conversation today — especially the discussion around {specific topic from meeting}.

As promised, here's {what was discussed: deck / data room link / metrics / intro}.

{1 sentence reinforcing the most compelling point from the meeting, ideally something they responded positively to.}

Looking forward to next steps. Happy to {provide additional info / schedule a follow-up / connect with references} — whatever would be most helpful for your process.

Best,
{sender_name}

---

## Notes
- Send within 24 hours of meeting
- Always reference something specific from the conversation
- Include any promised materials
- End with a clear but soft next step
- Keep it short — they already met you, no need to re-pitch
